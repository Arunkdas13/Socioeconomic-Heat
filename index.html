Methodology

Four steps. All public data. Directly extending your WACC decomposition to the deal level.

01
Deal-level WACC reconstruction
Apply equations 3–4 from your paper to each WB PPI transaction. Country risk (Damodaran default spread) + tech risk (maturity thresholds) + infrastructure premium → deal-level WACC estimate.

Deployment gap measure

Panel regression
